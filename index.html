How scoring and adaptation work
  • Unseen questions begin at 0% mastery rather than receiving credit for material you have not practiced.
  • Correct answers increase mastery; repeated misses, “unsure,” and “guessed” responses keep a concept in rotation.
  • Answer choices are reshuffled each time so letter-position memory cannot substitute for content knowledge.
  • “Make this harder” moves directly to a linked application question when one exists.
  • Adaptive sessions discourage back-to-back questions from the same domain or concept so one large topic does not dominate the session.
